    How it works

    Adoption in four easy steps

    We keep it simple and friendly, so you can focus on getting to know your new companion.

    1

    Browse pets

    Filter by type, age and shelter to find pets that suit your home.

    2

    Apply online

    Fill in a short form about you and your home. It takes about ten minutes.

    3

    Meet and greet

    Visit the shelter and spend time together before you decide.

    4

    Welcome home

    Take your new friend home, with follow up support from our team.
